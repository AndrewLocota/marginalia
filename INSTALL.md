# Install Marginalia on your Android phone

**[Download Marginalia 1.99 (302 KB)](https://github.com/AndrewLocota/marginalia/releases/download/v1.99/Marginalia-1.99-build13.apk)**

An APK is an Android app installer. You do not need Android Studio, a GitHub account, or any coding knowledge. This app is for Android phones (Android 8 or newer), not iPhones. It is downloaded from GitHub, not the Play Store.

## Install in a few taps

1. Open the download link above **on your Android phone**. If you are reading this on a computer, send the link to your phone.
2. Download `Marginalia-1.99-build13.apk`. On the release page, it is under **Assets**. Do not choose **Source code (zip)** or **Source code (tar.gz)**; those are for developers.
3. Tap the downloaded file, either from the browser's download message or your phone's **Files → Downloads** folder.
4. If Android says this browser or Files app cannot install apps, tap **Settings** and enable **Allow from this source** for that app, then return to the installer. Only do this for the Marginalia file you intentionally downloaded from this repository. You can turn this permission off afterward.
5. Tap **Install**, then **Open**. If you already have Marginalia installed, you may see **Update** instead. Normally you do not need to uninstall it.

Android may show a warning because this download is outside the Play Store. Keep Play Protect enabled. If installation is blocked, share the exact message in a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/AndrewLocota/marginalia/issues); do not disable your phone's security protections.

## Put a quote on your home screen

1. Open **Marginalia → Screens**.
2. Pick your style and how often the quote changes: every day or every 12 hours.
3. Tap **Add home-screen widget**, then confirm **Add** on your phone.
4. Hold the widget to move or resize it. Tap the quote for the author's emoji animation; tap the author to open its source.

If the Add button is unavailable, hold an empty part of your home screen, choose **Widgets**, find **Marginalia**, and drag its widget onto your screen.

## Optional: a quote on your lock screen

In **Screens**, turn on the **lock-screen quotation card** and allow notifications when asked. It keeps your existing wallpaper. Your phone's notification privacy settings decide whether quote text is visible while locked. Long passages may need you to expand the card.

Some phones also offer native lock-screen widgets. Availability varies; Android 16 alone does not guarantee this. The optional “lock screen only” notification mode needs additional Notification Access, explained inside the app; leave the normal notification mode selected if you do not want that access.

## Updating and getting help

Download a newer APK from [Releases](https://github.com/AndrewLocota/marginalia/releases) and open it to update. Avoid uninstalling first: that can remove your saved thoughts and settings. If Android reports a conflict, stop and report the message rather than deleting your app data.

Version 1.99, build 13 is an early GitHub release. It has been checked on Android 13 and 16 emulators; physical-phone compatibility and performance are not guaranteed. A Samsung storage-size reporting discrepancy remains unresolved. The APK download itself is about 302 KB. See the [README](README.md) for known limitations and quote-rights information.
