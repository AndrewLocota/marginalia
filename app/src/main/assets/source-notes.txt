MARGINALIA · SOURCES AND EDITORIAL NOTES

The selected collection focuses on knowledge, power, institutions, history, human nature, agency and limits. The full historical anthology remains available.

Every passage carries an edition, locator and link to the source text or a clearly identified excerpt. Text-fragment links highlight passages in browsers that support them; otherwise use the printed locator. External reading requires a browser and internet connection. The app itself works offline.

Source notes identify dramatic or fictional speakers, reported sayings, uncertain authorship, and excerpts from longer sentences. These are perspectives to examine, not claims endorsed by the app.

Whitespace and typographic emphasis are normalized. Quotation excerpts may capitalize the initial letter and end at a clause boundary; such changes are noted. Vico, Pessoa and Steinhardt use explicitly labelled editorial English translations with their source wording.

Modern works are represented by brief excerpts, not by redistributed full books. Historical editions are drawn chiefly from Project Gutenberg, the Internet Classics Archive, Perseus, and Wikisource. Publisher and author-foundation excerpts are identified individually.
